# CITIMART Daily Operations — Manager's Manual Data Entry User Guide

A step-by-step guide designed for store managers to easily understand how to log **Footfall**, **Billing Details**, **NOB**, and **Remarks**, and how daily submissions work.

---

## 1. Operating Hours & Time Stamp
- **Store Entry Hours**: `10:30 AM` to `11:59 PM` (IST).
- **Time Stamp**: Whenever you enter data, select the clock time when the activity occurred.
- **Time Slot**: The system automatically assigns your timestamp to the correct 3-hour operational band:
  - `11.00 AM - 01.59 PM` (includes morning opening from 10:30 AM)
  - `02.00 PM - 04.59 PM` (afternoon slot)
  - `05.00 PM - 07.59 PM` (evening slot)
  - `08.00 PM - 11.59 PM` (closing slot)
- *Note*: You do not have to calculate the slot manually; the system handles it automatically.

---

## 2. Footfall Entry (Customer Walk-ins)
- **What is Footfall?**
  Footfall is the total number of people/visitors who walked through the store doors during that time period, whether they bought something or not.
- **How to Log**:
  1. Set the **Time Stamp**.
  2. Type the visitor count in the **Footfall (visitors)** box (e.g., `45`).
  3. Click **"Update Footfall"**.
  4. The live Footfall KPI on your dashboard updates immediately.

---

## 3. Billing Details & NOB (Sales & Transactions)
Enter your sales counter data in this section:
- **Net Amount (₹)**:
  The total money collected from the bills in Rupees (e.g., `14500`).
- **Bill Quantity**:
  The total number of physical articles or items sold in those bills (e.g., `9`).
- **NOB (Number of Bills / Buyers)**:
  The number of customer receipts/transactions generated (e.g., `3`). Every bill represents 1 paying customer/buyer.
- **How to Log**:
  1. Set the **Time Stamp**.
  2. Enter **Net Amount**, **Bill Quantity**, and **NOB**.
  3. Click **"Update Bills & NOB"**.
  4. The dashboard updates your Net Sales, Conversion %, ATV (Average Transaction Value), and Basket Size instantly.

---

## 4. Remarks & Daily Observations
- **What are Remarks?**
  A brief note explaining any special factor that affected store performance that day.
  - Examples: *"Heavy rain from 3 PM to 5 PM"*, *"Festival shopping rush"*, *"Weekend discount promotion"*, *"AC malfunction for 1 hour"*.
- Remarks are saved with your daily record and included in executive reports.

---

## 5. "Update" vs "Final Submission"
| Button | When to Use | What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Update** (Footfall / Bills & NOB) | Throughout the day as shifts progress | Saves that section's entries so the live Dashboard shows up-to-the-minute figures. You can add more data anytime. |
| **Final Submission** | At the end of the day before leaving | Saves all remaining fields and locks in today's official daily summary snapshot and Remarks. |

> [!TIP]
> **Automatic Midnight Final Submission (Safety Net)**:
> If you ever forget to click the **Final Submission** button before leaving the store, don't worry! The system automatically finalizes your day's submission at **00:00 midnight**, ensuring none of your logged entries or calculations are lost.

---

## 6. Reviewing and Correcting Mistakes
If you made a typo in time, amount, or count:
1. Switch to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Scroll down to **"Logged Footfall"** and **"Logged Bills & NOB"**.
3. Click the single **Edit** button on any row to change any field (time, amount, quantity, NOB) and click **Save**.
4. Click **Delete** to remove a mistaken entry.
